Problem Statement

A grocery store wants to calculate the total cost of items purchased by a customer.

- Calculate the total cost of 3 different items
- Apply a 10% discount if total cost exceeds $50
- Display the final payable amount

---

Approach

1. Take input price of 3 items from the user
2. Calculate total amount
3. Check if total is greater than 50
4. Apply 10% discount if condition is true
5. Display original total, discount and final amount

---

Sample Output

Enter price of Item 1: 20
Enter price of Item 2: 25
Enter price of Item 3: 15

Original Total: 60
Discount: 6
Final Amount: 54

Conclusion

This program demonstrates the use of input/output operations, conditional statements and arithmetic operations in Python to simulate a real-life grocery billing system.
